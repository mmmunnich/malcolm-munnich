Modeling case study

xCatch Defensive Evaluation

Built an opportunity-based defensive model to estimate expected outcomes on balls in play and quantify observed performance relative to that expectation.

PythonkNN / classificationFieldingExpected outcomesRun values

Problem

Traditional fielding totals tell you what happened, but not how difficult each opportunity was. The model therefore starts with the opportunity and asks: given the measurable characteristics of the ball and context, how often would an average defender be expected to convert it?

Approach

1
Opportunity model
2
Observed vs expected
3
Run-value translation

The broader system joins batted-ball observations to box-score fielding events, estimates opportunity difficulty, calculates opportunity above expected, and translates those differences into run values.

Interpretation

OAE

How many opportunities a player converted above or below the model expectation.

RAA

A run-value representation of the defensive contribution after weighting outcomes.

Important limitations

  • Model performance depends on representative training opportunities.
  • Position-specific responsibilities need to be handled carefully.
  • Double-play and catcher-framing components are treated separately from the core fielding framework.
  • Proprietary tracking observations are not included in the public repository.
