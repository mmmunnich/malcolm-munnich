Player & Pitcher Scouting Tools

A group of interactive views for turning player-level baseball data into concise scouting summaries.

JavaScriptVisualizationScoutingInteractive analytics

Design goal

The goal is not to show every available metric. It is to help a user move quickly from raw observations to a few interpretable questions: what is this player doing, in which situations, and what changes when context changes?

Hitter views

Contact profile

Spray direction, contact quality, zone behavior, and performance summaries.

Context splits

Situational and count-based views designed for comparison rather than raw-data inspection.

Pitcher views

Pitch mix

Usage, movement, velocity, and pitch-type summaries.

Count & situation

Pitch selection and performance patterns by count and game context.

Data-protection approach

No raw workplace exports are distributed. Public demonstrations should use synthetic or explicitly authorized data. The portfolio describes the analytical structure while keeping proprietary observations private.
