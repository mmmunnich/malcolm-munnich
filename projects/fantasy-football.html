How the model works

Projected points

Expected fantasy production under configurable scoring.

Value over replacement

Compares players against a positional replacement level.

Scarcity + draft state

Accounts for positional cliffs and the alternatives still available.

Durability + simulation

Adjusts for expected games and simulates a 17-week season with weekly variance and injury absences.
